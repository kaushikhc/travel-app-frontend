Country and region names and codes (ISO 3166-1 and ISO 3166-2) come from the Debian iso-codes project (https://salsa.debian.org/iso-codes-team/iso-codes), as packaged by pycountry. iso-codes is licensed under the GNU Lesser General Public License, version 2.1 or later.

The country-to-continent mapping comes from pycountry-convert (MIT License), which is based on Wikipedia's "List of countries by continent" (Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0).
